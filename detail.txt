--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

AI City puts you in charge of a small futuristic city as its mayor. You do not
control the citizens directly. Each one is an autonomous agent with needs,
personality traits, skills, goals, memories and friendships. Each decides
for itself, minute by minute, whether to work, rest, eat, shop, socialise,
look for a job, get medical care or protest.

You shape the city indirectly through:
  - Construction: roads, housing, businesses, energy and public services
  - Economic policy: income tax, sales tax, unemployment benefit, energy
    conservation
  - Business subsidies and bailouts
  - Petitions: decisions citizens put to you, each with clear costs

The goal is to stay in office. Every 7 in-game days the citizens vote, and if
your approval rating is below your difficulty's threshold, you lose.

Design principles that run through the code:
  - The simulation is deterministic: seeded RNG, fixed timestep, pure
    TypeScript with no DOM.
  - Money is conserved: every credit moves through one ledgered transfer
    function.
  - Every event has a cause: events fire only from explicit state conditions
    and record the values that triggered them.
  - Everything can be explained: happiness, approval and citizen decisions
    are itemised and shown in the UI.

4. GAMEPLAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Starting a game
  A "Welcome, Mayor" modal lets you pick a difficulty and an optional seed
  (the same seed gives the same city), then "Found the city".

  Difficulty    Starting treasury    Re-election needs
  ----------    -----------------    -----------------
  Relaxed       9,000 cr             30% approval
  Standard      6,000 cr             40% approval
  Hard          3,500 cr             50% approval

  The starting city is a 40 x 26 tile map with 20 citizens, 14 of them
  employed, and this layout:
    - West residential: 4 Houses and 1 Apartment Tower
    - Civic centre: Park, City Hall, Clinic
    - Commercial: Office, Shop, Market Hall
    - Industry and energy: Fabricator (factory), Fusion Plant
  Essential services are staffed first. Remaining workers are spread
  round-robin across businesses, and each job goes to the best-skilled
  candidate.

4.2 Time
  - 1 tick = 5 in-game minutes. 12 ticks = 1 hour, 288 ticks = 1 day.
  - The game starts on Day 1 (a Monday) at 06:00.
  - Base step 125 ms of real time per tick at 1x speed. Speeds: 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x.
  - A fixed-step clock with a catch-up cap makes simulation speed independent
    of frame rate. The renderer interpolates movement between ticks.
  - Work hours are 07:00-19:00 with an 8-hour shift. Offices and fabricators
    close at weekends. Shops, services and the power plant keep running, and
    citizens shop and socialise more at weekends.

4.3 Elections and approval
  An election is held every 7 days (TERM_DAYS). Approval moves 12% of the way
  toward a target value each hour. The target is the sum of these itemised
  factors:
    Baseline 50
    Citizen mood            (avg happiness - 60) x 0.9
    Unemployment > 5%       -(rate - 0.05) x 70
    Homeless                -3 each (max -15)
    Blackouts               -8
    Closed businesses       -3 each (max -9)
    Protest at City Hall    -10
    Budget crisis           -6
    High income tax (>15%)  -(rate - 0.15) x 60
    Low income tax (<10%)   +(0.10 - rate) x 30
    High sales tax (>10%)   -(rate - 0.10) x 40
    Generous benefits       +2 (benefit >= 15 and some unemployed)
    Working clinic          +2   / no working clinic -5
    Plenty of parks         +2 (1 park per 15 citizens)
    Public school           +2
    Temporary modifiers from petitions and promises
  - Win: your term number rises and you receive a grant of
    500 + 25 x population credits.
  - Lose: game over. You can start a new city or keep playing in sandbox
    mode, which has no more elections.

4.4 Petitions (decisions put to the mayor)
  Up to 2 petitions can be open at once. None appear during the first hours
  of a new city. If you don't answer before a petition expires, its default
  option applies. The game can auto-pause while a petition is waiting; this
  is a toggle under the event log.

  Petition            Trigger                     Options
  -----------------   -------------------------   ------------------------------
  Bailout             Business distressed 3+ h    Pay 400 cr (+3 approval for a
                                                  day) / let it close
  Emergency power     Sustained power shortage    Buy 250 cr: +25 supply for
                                                  24 h / decline
  Street festival     Weekend 09-13, pop >= 10,   Sponsor 300 cr: +6 mood until
                      happiness >= 45, no storm   23:00, +4 approval for 2 days /
                                                  skip
  Tax relief          Income tax > 18% and avg    Cut to 15%: +5 approval for
                      happiness < 60              2 days / refuse: -5
  Cooling centers     Heatwave                    Open 150 cr: no heat health or
                                                  mood loss / ignore: -3
  Park request        40%+ of housed citizens     Promise a park within 3 days
                      have no park nearby         (kept: +6, broken: -8) /
                                                  decline: -3

4.5 Milestones and city rank
  Ranks: Settlement -> Town -> City -> Metropolis. Each milestone pays a
  regional grant.

  Milestone            Requirement                                     Reward
  -------------------  ----------------------------------------------  ------
  Groundbreaking       Construct your first new building                 200
  Clean Energy         Build a Solar Array                               300
  Town Charter         30 citizens (rank Town, unlocks School)           600
  Full Employment      Unemployment < 5% with >= 25 citizens             400
  Thriving Community   Average happiness >= 70 for 24 hours              500
  Balanced Books       3 consecutive days with a budget surplus          500
  Second Term          Win your first re-election                        300
  Knowledge City       Open a School                                     300
  City Charter         50 citizens (rank City)                          1200
  Green City           3 parks and no home with polluted air             600
  Metropolis           80 citizens (rank Metropolis)                    2500

4.6 The Morning Circuit (daily newspaper)
  Published every day at 06:00 from day 2 onward. It covers the biggest news
  of the last 24 hours, key figures with their daily change, a citizen
  spotlight and the weather forecast.

4.7 Weather
  Seeded weather fronts with a known next front, which drives the forecast.

  Kind          Solar   Power demand   Mood   Outdoor   Duration (h)
  -----------   -----   ------------   ----   -------   ------------
  Clear         1.00    x1.0           +2     +0.1      6-14
  Overcast      0.55    x1.0            0      0        4-10
  Rain          0.35    x1.0           -2     -0.3      3-8
  Thunderstorm  0.20    x1.0           -4     -0.7      2-5
  Heatwave      1.00    x1.2           -5     -0.2      8-20
  A heatwave also costs health unless cooling centers are open.

6. SIMULATION MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 Citizens
  Each citizen has:
    - Name, age, occupation, money, wage, food at home
    - Traits (0-1): sociability, diligence, frugality, resilience
    - Skills: technical, manual, social (these decide hiring and wages)
    - Needs: energy, hunger, health, happiness
    - Goals: saveMoney, findJob, makeFriends, stayHealthy
    - Memories (up to 30) with emotional impact
    - Relationships with other citizens (friendship scores)
    - The last decision record: chosen action, reasons, runner-up scores
    - Itemised happiness factors

6.2 Decision making (decision.ts)
  Each idle citizen scores every feasible (action, target) pair and commits
  to the best one. Actions: work, rest, eat, shop, socialize, seekJob,
  seekCare, protest, wait. Scores combine needs, personality, goals, money,
  time of day, weekend, weather, travel time (penalty 0.004 per tick), friends
  and a small random noise. Unreachable targets are never chosen. The
  inspector shows why a citizen chose what it did.

6.3 Activities (activities.ts)
  State machine: idle -> traveling -> performing -> idle. Paths are re-planned
  when the map changes. A trip longer than 240 ticks is abandoned. Citizens
  standing on a newly built tile are moved out, so no one gets stuck.

6.4 Needs and happiness (needs.ts)
  Energy decays (faster at work). Hunger grows (more slowly while resting).
  Starvation and exhaustion damage health. Health recovers naturally, and
  quickly at a clinic. Pollution and heat damage health.
  Happiness drifts toward an itemised target. Baseline 45, plus:
    employed +6 / unemployed -6 to -20, homeless -20, no power at home -10,
    hungry, exhausted, unwell, friends (up to +8), recent socialising (up
    to +9), polluted air (up to -14), park nearby +6, school nearby +3, no
    clinic nearby -3, long commute (up to -6), weather, weekend +3, festival,
    nearly broke (-8), and others.

6.5 Economy (economy.ts)
  - Every transfer goes through transfer(), which refuses overdrafts. Only
    the outside-world account may go negative.
  - Wages are paid per worked tick. Income and sales tax go to the treasury.
  - Residents pay daily rent and a utilities bill of 3 cr.
  - Retail prices: meal 10, grocery 7, luxury 18. Wealthier citizens spend
    more on luxuries.
  - Supply chain: shops restock from the local Fabricator at a wholesale
    price of 2.5, or import at 5.5. Factory surplus is exported at 2.2.
  - Businesses pay operating costs and power fees. A business that stays
    insolvent for 12 hours closes and lays off its staff. A subsidy (600 cr)
    or bailout reopens it.
  - Public buildings and roads cost daily upkeep from the treasury.
  - Wages are reviewed daily. Schools raise skills, and with them wages.

6.6 Power (power.ts)
  Supply comes from plants, solar and imported power. Demand rises 20% in a
  heatwave for homes and businesses, and energy conservation cuts it by 20%.
  Shortages are distributed by priority. Unpowered homes hurt mood, and
  unpowered businesses stop working.

6.7 Environment (environment.ts)
  Pollution from running factories (strength 1, radius 7) and the fusion
  plant (0.5, radius 5). Parks clean the air (0.3, radius 5). Service
  coverage radii: park 10, clinic 18, school 12. Commutes longer than 24
  tiles lower mood.

6.8 Population (population.ts)
  Every 6 hours a newcomer arrives at the road on the map edge if there are
  vacant homes, average happiness is at least 55, and there are open jobs or
  at least 3 vacant homes. A citizen whose happiness stays below 15 for 12
  hours emigrates.

6.9 Events (events.ts)
  Detected with hysteresis and streak counters. Each event lists its causes,
  for example: power shortage and restoration, citizen complaints (hunger,
  homelessness, blackouts, unpaid wages, no work, sickness without care),
  protests and their dispersal, high or rising unemployment, goods shortage,
  budget crisis, housing shortage, public health crisis, business closures
  and reopenings, policy changes, milestones, elections and petitions.
  The game can auto-pause on critical events (optional).

6.10 Tick orchestration (engine.ts: stepWorld)
  Every tick:   power update and events, need decay, activity steps, decisions
                for idle citizens (sharing distance fields per tile), social
                interactions, happiness drift.
  Every hour:   weather, effect expiry, restocking, exports, business costs,
                upkeep, solvency/closures, environmental health, school
                learning (08-18), goals, happiness factors, emigration,
                immigration (every 6 h), hourly events, petitions,
                milestones, approval, stats sample, newspaper at 06:00.
  Every day:    rent and benefits, daily stats reset, wage review,
                surplus streak, unemployment trend, election check.

8. USER INTERFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Top bar     Approval (vs. needed), population, treasury (with daily
              change), employment, happiness, energy supply/demand,
              weather, day/clock, pause/speed buttons, Save/Load/New game
  Toolbar     Inspect, Road, Demolish, and buildings grouped by category
              (Housing, Commerce, Industry, Energy, Public) with costs and
              lock reasons
  Map         Phaser-rendered city with citizens, day/night lighting and
              overlays
  Sidebar     Tabs:
                Inspect   - citizen (needs, decision reasons, memories,
                            friends, goals) or building (staff, finances,
                            power, subsidy/demolish)
                Mayor     - approval breakdown, election countdown,
                            petitions, milestones and rank
                Citizens  - list of all citizens
                Stats     - city statistics and history
                Policies  - tax rates, benefit, energy conservation
  Event log   Filterable by severity (All / Warnings / Critical), with
              causes and links to the citizens and buildings involved.
              Toggles for pause-on-critical and pause-on-petition.
  Overlays    Petition cards, the daily newspaper card, toasts
  Modals      New game (welcome), game over

Policy limits: income tax 0-35% (default 15%), sales tax 0-20% (default 8%),
unemployment benefit 0-40 cr/day (default 10), energy conservation on/off
(default off).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. SAVING AND LOADING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Saved to browser localStorage under the key "ai-city:save:slot1".
  - Format: { format, version, savedAt, world }. The current version is 2.
  - Version 1 saves are migrated automatically (MIGRATIONS in
    persistence.ts).
  - Loading validates the whole structure (ids, references, ranges,
    capacities, overlaps, petitions, effects). If anything is invalid, the
    running game is left untouched.
  - "ai-city:intro-seen" remembers whether the welcome screen has been shown.

11. ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ React UI ]  <-- coarse updates (~200 ms) --  [ GameController ]
       |                                           |  owns World + clock
       +-- commands (build, policy, answer) -----> |
                                                   |  frame(): converts real
  [ Phaser CityScene ] <-- reads world each frame  |  time to ticks
       +-- input forwarded to the controller ----> |
                                                   v
                                          stepWorld(world)  (src/sim)

  - The simulation lives in src/sim. It is pure, deterministic and
    serialisable to plain JSON, and it can be tested with no browser.
  - The world advances only through stepWorld(world).
  - The renderer never mutates the simulation. It forwards input to the
    controller.
  - Commands such as placement, demolition, policies and subsidies are
    validated and atomic, and return { ok, reason }.
